# StoreLab (working name): one page

**Verdict: Not yet.** Each store-month makes money before fixed costs, but year 1 is a loss and too few simulated buyers buy. Details are in `business-plan.md`.

**The problem.** Philippine mini-mart chains change endcaps, shelves and checkout displays all the time, but their trials have no proper control stores. Nobody can say whether a change actually sold more. A wrong roll-out costs labour and sales in every store.

**The customer.** Heads of operations or merchandising at mid-size Philippine convenience and mini-mart chains (60–200 stores) who already run small trials.

**The offer and price.**
- **The 8-Week Store Test: PHP 75,000.** We recommend one change, simulate it on the chain's own camera and POS data, run it in 2–3 test stores against matched control stores, and report the result in pesos. Full refund if the test stores don't beat the controls on a KPI the chain sets in writing. A free camera check comes before signing.
- **Then Core: PHP 3,000 per store per month**, month-to-month. The pilot fee is credited against the first 6 months.

**The numbers** (all PHP; estimates):

| key number | |
| --- | ---: |
| Price | 3,000.00 a store-month |
| Profit margin at plan | 17% per store-month |
| Break-even | 120 store-months a month |
| Year 1 operating profit | -2,995,200 |
| Startup spend | 400,000 |
| Cash needed before it pays for itself | 3,395,200 |
| Startup money earned back | not in year 1 |
| Buyer panel | 2 buy · 18 pass |

On the lean plan (one paid engineer, founders unpaid in year 1), break-even is 60 stores and year 1 is a loss of 1,195,200 (`cfo.md`).

**The evidence.**
- **Buyer panel (simulated):** 2 of 20 would buy (10%), up from 0 of 20 for a 12-month PHP 8,000/store contract. Trust is the blocker, not price.
- **Competitor gap:** existing tools either measure traffic (people counters, CCTV analytics) or test layouts in online virtual stores. None in the sources combines the chain's own cameras, simulation and a test-vs-control pilot plan for Philippine chains. The gap is narrow and copyable.
- **Real test:** not run yet. A working software prototype exists, on synthetic data.

**What the money is for.** About **PHP 1.6M (lean)** to **PHP 3.4M (full team)** covers registration, the Data Privacy Act opinion and DPIA, pilot kits, one engineer and runway until about 60 paying stores.

**The ask.** Help us reach **3 paid pilots by 31 December 2026**: an introduction to a chain's operations head, or a brand willing to co-fund a test of its own endcap. Funding conversations come after the first readout.

*The panel is simulated buyers; every number is a projection from estimates. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
