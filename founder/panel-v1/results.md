## What would flip a no

- A paid pilot on 3 to 5 stores for 2-3 months with no 12-month lock-in, where the money-back depends on the predicted sales lift actually showing up against control stores.
- A short paid or free pilot on a few of our stores with no 12-month lock-in, where their predicted lift is checked against our real control stores before any contract.
- A free or paid-on-results pilot in 2-3 of our stores for 2-3 months with no 12-month lock-in, plus a written Data Privacy Act compliance opinion I can show our DPO.
- A free or paid-on-results pilot in a few of our stores where the simulated prediction actually matched what happened at the till, with no 12-month contract until that is proven.
- A free or paid-by-results pilot in two or three of my stores against control stores that shows a sales lift covering the fee, with no 12-month lock-in.
- A paid pilot on 2-3 of my stores for 2-3 months, no 12-month lock-in, where I only continue if real POS sales in the test stores beat the control stores by more than the fee.
- Seeing a big chain like 7-Eleven or Alfamart get real results with it, or a free pilot on 2-3 of my stores using our old cameras with no 12-month lock-in.
- A paid pilot in 2-3 of my stores for 2-3 months with no long contract, plus a written Data Privacy Act compliance opinion, and they show me the predicted lift actually happened against my control stores.
- A paid pilot on 2 or 3 of my stores for 2-3 months with no annual lock-in, where I only continue if the test stores beat the control stores by more than the fee.
- A short paid pilot on 2-3 of my stores with no 12-month lock-in, where StoreLab predicts the result of a layout change before we make it and the real POS numbers from test vs control stores confirm it.
- A free or very cheap pilot in 2-3 of my stores for a couple of months, no 12-month lock-in, where their recommended layout actually beats my control stores on sales.
- A free or brand-funded pilot in a few of my stores, with written Data Privacy Act clearance, that shows a real sales lift against control stores before I commit to any contract.
